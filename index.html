01 · Letter

From the CFO, to the reader.

BasedKaohsiung, Taiwan LanguagesSpanish, English, German, Mandarin, Italian StandardsUS GAAP · IFRS · JGAAP · HK GAAP

For thirteen years I have run finance where it is hardest: multi-entity groups, several jurisdictions, and moments when the numbers decide whether a company is funded, sold or saved. The last eight of those years have been in Asia, across Japan, Hong Kong and Korea.

Today I am CFO of a Japanese hospitality investment group with more than US$200M in assets across 13 properties. In 2026 I led two investor due diligences: a ¥200M strategic corporate investment at a ¥2.7B valuation, built on a business plan I wrote myself, and an ongoing liquidity event that remains confidential.

Before that I turned two businesses around, grew EBT by 4,500% at a start-up and supported its owner's exit, and in Europe I completed four acquisitions, modelled an €80M syndicated loan and unified reporting for ten countries.

What sets my finance function apart is how it works. I build our tools with AI myself, I made AI training mandatory for the team, and a management report now takes under thirty seconds. I am a Certified Fraud Examiner, so every one of those tools is built with controls first.
